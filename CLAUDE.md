# Standing rules

## Honesty

Royto has no delivered client work yet. Until it does: no testimonials, no
client logos, no case studies, no invented metrics, and no language implying
current clients ("delivering", "in pilot", "our clients", "we've helped").
Simulated UI carries a visible "Example workflow" label. Roadmap items are
visibly marked as roadmap.

## Copy

All copy lives in `src/content/` and comes verbatim from
`../attachments/copy.md`. Do not name the founder's employer anywhere on the
site.

Voice: **we** for what Royto does, builds, includes and prices (hero sub,
capabilities, How it works, Websites, Approval, Products, Pricing, FAQ
answers). **I** only when the founder makes a promise to a person (the
founding-client line, About, the contact lede and reply promise, the Royto
Social pilot invitation). Status vocabulary is exactly two labels: "Available
now" and "On the roadmap".
